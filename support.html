Frequently asked questions

Does everyone in the group need the App?

No. The organizer can add travelers by nickname and maintain the complete ledger.

Does Trip Ledger send or request money?

No. It records expenses, calculates balances, and suggests settlement transfers. It does not connect to a bank or move money.

What is the difference between Paid and Fair share?

Paid is the amount a traveler advanced. Fair share is the amount that traveler is responsible for based on the expense splits. The difference determines whether that traveler should pay or receive money.

What do households change?

A household combines its members only when suggesting final transfers. It does not change who paid or each person's share of an expense, and Trip Ledger does not suggest transfers within the household.

Can I use more than one currency?

Yes. Keep the original amount and convert it to the Trip's settlement currency using an actual card charge, a manual rate, a saved Trip rate, or an optional Bank of Canada reference rate. Existing expenses never silently change when a later rate becomes available.

How do I correct an exchange rate?

Open the foreign-currency expense, choose to use or change to a new rate, review the new settlement amount, and confirm the reconversion. Nothing changes before confirmation.

Is a PDF report a backup?

No. A PDF is readable but cannot reconstruct the App's ledger. Use Prepare Full Backup from Backup and Restore when you need a restorable copy.

What happens when I restore a backup?

Restore replaces all current Trip Ledger data; it does not merge. The App shows a preview and creates an automatic backup of current data before replacement. Review Trip, traveler, and expense counts carefully.

How do I restore Trip Ledger Pro?

Open Purchases from the More menu and choose Restore Purchase while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple handles purchases and refunds. Existing trips remain accessible even if the Pro entitlement changes.

Can support recover a deleted Trip?

No. Trip Ledger does not keep a server copy. If you previously created a full backup, you may restore it, but remember that a restore replaces all current trips.
