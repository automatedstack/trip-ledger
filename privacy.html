Privacy Policy

Trip Ledger is designed to keep your travel ledger on your device and under your control.

Summary

  • No Trip Ledger account is required.
  • We do not operate advertising, analytics, or tracking systems in the App.
  • We do not receive your trips, traveler nicknames, expenses, balances, payment history, or backups.
  • You decide whether to export or share a report or backup.

Information stored on your device

Trip Ledger stores the information you enter—such as trip names, dates, settlement currencies, traveler nicknames, households, expense descriptions and categories, amounts, payers, shares, exchange-rate facts, settlement suggestions, and completed-payment records—in the App's local container on your device. App preferences and purchase entitlement state are also stored locally. We do not operate a server that receives this ledger information.

Exports and backups

PDF reports and complete JSON backups are created locally. They leave the App only when you choose a destination through Apple's document picker or Share Sheet. After export, the destination service you select—such as Files, email, messaging, or cloud storage—handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. A complete backup can contain traveler nicknames, descriptions, notes, and financial facts, so protect it as you would other sensitive financial records.

Reference exchange rates

Manual exchange rates work without a network connection. If you request a reference rate, Trip Ledger contacts the Bank of Canada Valet API and sends the requested currency pair and date window. The request does not include your trip name, traveler nicknames, expense description, expense amount, balances, or backup data. Like other internet services, the Bank of Canada receives normal network information such as your IP address and may log request details. Trip Ledger's developer does not receive or control those logs. See the Bank of Canada website privacy practices and terms of use.

Purchases

Trip Ledger Pro is offered through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the transaction; Trip Ledger does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple may provide the App with transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock or restore the purchase. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's policies.

Permissions and data we do not access

V1 does not request access to contacts, location, photos, microphone, or bank accounts. The App does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Your ledger remains on your device until you delete records, delete the App, erase the device, or restore a different complete backup. Some deleted accounting records may be retained locally inside the ledger to preserve history, reversals, or data integrity while being excluded from active totals. Externally exported files must be deleted from their destination separately.

Children

Trip Ledger is a general travel utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children because we do not operate an account or data-collection service.

Security

Trip Ledger relies on iOS application-container protections. No method of storage is completely risk-free. Use a device passcode, keep iOS current, and store exported backups only in locations you trust.

Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before the changed version is released. The effective date at the top identifies the current policy version.
